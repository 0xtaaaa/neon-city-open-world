HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Shift to sprint.
Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A D steer, Space handbrake.
E near a car steals it. E at the yellow ◆ starts a mission.
E at a shop door opens it: gunsmith (weapons), spray shop (repair / paint).
E at the checkered flag starts a street race ($100 entry).
Crimes raise your wanted level — lose the cops by breaking line of sight.
M changes the radio station, N mutes. Progress saves automatically.
Earn respect to unlock missions and climb: Nobody → Hustler → Street → Made → Kingpin → Legend.
